Situated in a beautiful nature preserve within the heart of Silicon Valley, Shoreline Lake is an antidote to the hectic pace of life. At this relaxing waterfront respite – one of the San Francisco Bay Area's best kept secrets – everyone can take advantage of both a tranquil oasis and the atmosphere of a classic recreational resort.
