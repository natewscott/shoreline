Yearly Sale of Cool Stuff!

September 27th and 28th, 10am-4pm

About once per year, Shoreline Lake provides a great opportunity to get your hands on some fun, gently-used and well-maintained gear for a fraction of the retail price. Plus, the Gear Sale is scheduled so new owners have the chance to enjoy their purchases throughout early Fall and Indian Summer.

Currently, this year’s offerings can benefit both adults and kids looking for their own gear, and include kayaks, windsurfing gear, and stand up paddleboards. Miscellaneous kitchen equipment/supplies will also be available for purchase. Not sure which options would be a fit? Shoreline Lake staff will be at the ready to answer questions and assist buyers in getting exactly what they need.

Don't forget to check back (items listed can change), as the sale date gets closer!

Can't Make the Sale?

If interested in a piece of gear ahead of the sale, or the dates don't work for you, to reserve or purchase your desired item(s) outside of the sale timeframe, please contact the Boathouse Manager (650-965-7474 or boathouse@shorelinelake.com) directly.

And, the Boathouse Manager can also assist with inquiries about purchasing equipment not currently listed as part of the sale.
