Basic Wingfoiling

Take the next step in your wingfoiling experience! In this class, students will start out riding a foilboard while being towed by a powerboat to learn how to ride high above the water using a hydrofoil.

After learning the basics of riding the foilboard while being towed, students will progress to using the wing and foilboard together.

This class includes a lunch break (approximately 1 hour), and total instructional time is approximately 5 hours.

Prerequisites: Successful completion of Beginner or Intro Wingboarding course (or its equivalent). Students must be comfortable swimming and treading water.

What to Bring

Students should bring comfortable clothes, a bathing suit to wear under the wetsuit, a jacket or fleece, a towel, lots of sun protection, a lanyard to secure their glasses around their heads, a reusable water bottle, and shoes or sandals that can be worn in the water (such as water shoes/aqua socks or Keen sandals).

Important: Please arrive 10-15 minutes before your class to check in.
