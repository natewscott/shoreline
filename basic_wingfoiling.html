Basic Wingfoiling

Take the next step in your wingfoiling experience! In this class, students will ride a wingboard while being towed by a powerboat to learn how to ride high above the water using a hydrofoil.

Experience handling a wing on a non-foil board (in the Beginner Wingboarding/Intro Wingboaring class or equivalent) is required for this class. This towfoiling class does not involve use of the wing, as the powerboat and tow rope are substituted for a wing as your means of propulsion, but is intended to prepare students to use the wing and foil together in a later class.

Prerequisites: Successful completion of Intro Wingboarding course (or its equivalent). Students must be comfortable swimming and treading water.

What to Bring

Students should bring comfortable clothes, a bathing suit to wear under the wetsuit, a jacket or fleece, a towel, lots of sun protection, a lanyard to secure their glasses around their heads, a bottle of water, and non-skid/non-marking shoes to wear while windsurfing (they will get WET – tennis shoes, aqua socks, or Teva sandals work great).

Important: Please arrive 10-15 minutes before your class to check in.
