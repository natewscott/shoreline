Pressroom

Shoreline Lake is one of the few places that offers a true respite in Silicon Valley. Well worth visiting, it's the perfect place for a break from the office, lunch, or a few hours spent learning how to sail.

With a diverse range of classes, equipment for rent, and activities, Shoreline Lake's Boathouse and American Bistro has something to offer everyone. Of course, no requirement exists to do anything other than enjoy the view and take in the tranquility that is Shoreline Lake.

If you would like to get more background information though, as well as objective third-party perspectives, on Shoreline's many activities (and possibilities), please see the news stories and organizational information below.

For press-related inquiries, please contact Maria Gonzalez, of the Gonzberg Agency, at (415) 930-9103 or by email.
