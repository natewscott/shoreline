Meetings at Shoreline Lake

Situated in a tranquil wildlife sanctuary, within the heart of Silicon Valley, Shoreline Lake’s meeting facilities not only give participants a true respite from the office, but also create an environment conducive to productivity.

The Events Team – highly experienced in customizing and assisting with all manner of formal and informal gatherings – works with companies, church groups, associations, private groups and other organizations to ensure their teams get the maximum benefit from being offsite.

Whether the goal is to hash out budgets, engage in project planning, hold seminars and workshops, or take part in the myriad of other customized gatherings, this unique locale offers a number of advantages for running successful meetings. A few being:

  • Located between the Boathouse and the American Bistro, the primary meeting room offers a relaxing and panoramic waterfront view of the 50-acre sailing lake and surrounding parklands.
  • Overseen by Shoreline Lake’s Executive Chef, delicious on-site catering is available from a variety of different menu types, as well as seating inside, or outside on the American Bistro’s spacious lakeside patio.
  • This quiet oasis, easily accessed from US 101, and with ample parking, is a favorite for many employees of the best-known companies in Silicon Valley. (Centrally located in the Mid-Peninsula/South Bay, urban areas are just outside the park.)

In addition, Shoreline offers Teambuilding options that can also be incorporated into your team’s day. Plus, to reward them for their hard work and provide a relaxing end-of-day, your group can also have the option to hop on a pedalboat, canoe, or kayak, and paddle around the lake to clear their heads, and take in the natural surroundings and fresh air, before going home.
