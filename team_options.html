Team Building

Shoreline Lake offers a variety of orchestrated corporate team building and outdoor game options. Companies and other organizations wanting to provide their staff with unique experiences and gatherings can find plenty of alternatives here, along with “traditional” ones, that allow them to do something a little different, as well as social.

In addition, the extensive range of programs can be tailored to suit your needs and those of your staff. Whether in need of an entertaining hour of fun and laughter for employees and their families at your company picnic, a custom designed team bonding and communications workshop that enhances your team’s performance, or even a combination of both, Shoreline can put something together exactly to your liking.

Chances are Shoreline’s skilled and accomplished facilitators, together with your team, will create an experience that leaves your employees smiling – and results in other benefits to your company for years to come. Just choose from the options listed below, and contact Shoreline’s Events Team to organize and take care of the details.

Boat Building

⌄

    We offer a fun and creative team-oriented boat building competition for approximately 1.5 hours where participants are divided into teams and instructed to design and build a boat from unconventional materials. Teams must communicate and work together in order to build their boat before the timeframe runs out. Races are held at the end to determine whose boat design is indeed seaworthy! (10 people minimum)

Kayak Polo

⌄

    Groups are split into two opposing teams, for a fun and dynamic game of kayak polo. Once a person catches the ball they can no longer paddle towards the opposing team’s goal and must determine whether they should pass or shoot from where they float. It is like basketball on water – just don’t lose your balance. (Maximum of 20 people)

Water Activities

⌄

    Passes and organized activities that allow your team/group to access different watercraft*, over a specified timeframe. PFDs (life jackets) are included for each pass type/activity.

    Basic Access Pass

    All access to pedalboats, canoes, rowboats, and kayaks.

    Passes are for a 2-hour duration. With catering, passes may be used during your event. Price per person $22 (minimum group size required).

    Enhanced Access Pass

    Adds all access to Standup Paddleboards and Beach Cruiser bicycles, in addition to watercraft noted in Basic pass.

    Passes are for a 2-hour duration. With catering, passes may be used during your event. Price per person $25 (minimum group size required).


    Sailboat Rides

    Includes a skipper, for a 20-minute sail around the lake. Price per person $30 (minimum 2 persons).

    * Those interested in kayaking and standup paddleboarding may get wet. We suggest bringing swim shorts/bathing suits or other appropriate gear for the water, as well as a towel/change of clothes. .

Educational Wine Seminar

⌄

    Sip, Savor, and Strengthen: The perfect motto for our unique team building-based educational wine seminar. An engaging way to enhance your team’s collaboration and communication skills, this unique experience offers a perfect opportunity for learning, relaxation, and team bonding.

    Led by expert sommeliers, this immersive wine-tasting adventure takes your team through the art and science of wine – covering everything from wine regions and varietals, to tasting techniques and food pairings.

    While exploring the world of wine, your team will engage in fun, interactive activities designed to spark conversation, encourage collaboration, and build stronger relationships. Using group challenges (such as blind tastings, flavor identification, and pairing exercises), your team will enjoy the rich diverse world of wine, while developing valuable skills in decision-making, critical thinking, and communication.

    Offering a memorable way to grow together, our Educational Wine Seminar, besides raising a glass to teamwork, creativity and new experiences, also makes a distinctive way to celebrate a milestone, host a retreat and/or foster stronger connections in the workplace with your team.
