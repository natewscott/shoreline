Non-Profit Paddle Days

We are proud to offer a unique opportunity for non-profit organizations. For only $25 per person, groups of 25-200 people can enjoy three hours of boating on our canoes, pedalboats, kayaks, and rowboats. The cost includes lifejackets.

This is a great way for churches, scout troops, youth organizations, seniors’ clubs, and any other non-profit group to enjoy the tranquility of Shoreline Lake!

Price applies equally to a child or an adult. Minimum age is 2 years old.
