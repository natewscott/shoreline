Spring

Kayak & Sail Spring Camp

Spend this spring in the sun while enjoying a private waterfront in a team-oriented adventure. Introduce your kids to lifelong watersports by learning to kayak and sail.

Shoreline’s certified instructors provide a supportive learning environment and teach water safety, boat handling basics and even knot tying. Daily lessons and on-the-water exercises progressively develop each skill, and friends will learn to work cohesively as a team.

Shoreline Lake holds the highest US Sailing standards as our own with priorities encompassing Safety, Fun, and Learning. In this weeklong course, students will learn fundamental boating to prepare them for a lifetime of fun opportunities.

What to Bring

Students should bring comfortable clothes, a jacket or fleece, a towel and change of clothes, lots of sun protection, a lanyard to secure their glasses around their heads, a bottle of water, and shoes that stay on your feet and can get wet.

Please bring a bag lunch each day. Alternatively, the Bistro is also open for campers to purchase lunch (not included in cost of camp).

Important:On the first day of camp, please arrive 15-20 minutes before the start time to register and sign-in

Please Note:Also, parents, no pets, including dogs, may enter the park, even if they are inside a vehicle when dropping off and picking up campers.

Prerequisites: Ability to swim, minimum of 60 lbs.
