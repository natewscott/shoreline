Private Lessons

Shoreline Lake also provides private lessons, offering both individual and group options for all watersports classes. Private watersports clinics, in addition, are available for companies and other organizations, as well as outings for family and friends. The following watersports are available as a private clinic version:

  • Sailing
  • Windsurfing
  • Kayaking
  • Stand Up Paddleboarding
INDIVIDUAL & GROUP LESSONS INFO

Private lessons make a great option in case you need some one-on-one work with an expert to perfect that jibe, delve into the nuances of racing tactics, or refine that stroke.

These lessons are aimed at meeting the needs of more advanced enthusiasts who have taken a group lesson or have gained experience on their own. Aside from acting as a replacement for group classes, they also allow for close up, one-on-one instructor demonstrations and hands-on feedback. And, they are, in addition, a good option if you have scheduling conflicts or want to organize a special group event.

Please Note: Although private lessons can be a good way to get introduced to a sport, they are not a direct alternative for the intro classes (For introductory private lessons, we do not sell one hour sessions, as such a short lesson tends to breed frustration more than enjoyment of the sport.).

Lessons can be purchased in increments of 1-4 hours, with pricing noted below. Introductory or first-time private lessons typically require a 2-hour minimum.

PRIVATE WATERSPORTS CLINICS

Private Watersports Clinics are designed for all skill levels (with a focus on enjoying the activity, while learning something new), besides a unique team building experience, they make a great option for groups that want something a little different and educational, as well as social.

Private Sailing Clinics

Rigging, terminology...all of that can come later. Set up as a beginner clinic, the objective here is to show how engaging and accessible sailing can be for everyone, while learning the basics from experienced US Sailing-certified instructors.

In short, with this Clinic no sailing experience is required. Anyone who just wants to get out on the water, and relax in the sun with a calm breeze blowing, has license to find out what all the fuss is about, including why sailing is so much fun.

Private Windsurfing Clinics

A dedicated private lesson, tailored to each individual in the group, and designed for those wanting their first taste of windsurfing. By course’s end, everyone will be enjoying windsurfing back and forth across the lake.

Taking into account each participant’s skill level, experienced instructors (certified through US Sailing's Windsurfing Instructor Programs) ensure everyone can follow the curriculum in a manner custom-fit for the group.

Private Kayaking Clinics

No pressure to shoot down rapids during this Clinic. Instead, beginners learn skills that allow them to explore the lake comfortably with others in their group, and that, overall, lead to a greater enjoyment of being outdoors.

Paddlesport-certified instructors (with years of experience in advanced white-water and sea kayaking environments) address each participant’s skill level – making sure everyone can have fun, and feel relaxed, while taking part.

Private SUP Clinics

New to watersports, and want a great way to “surf” Shoreline Lake, no waves required? With easy-to-learn basics, and the chances to have fun exploring at a leisurely pace, this Clinic helps everyone take advantage of the lake’s safe and calm waters.

Tailored to meet the needs of all group members, regardless of skill level, experienced standup paddleboarding-certified instructors provide a personal touch that ensure each participant learns the techniques needed, so they can glide across the water.
