Performance Sailing – The Final Stage

For the serious youth sailor interested in polishing skills and learning to compete, this camp covers everything needed in preparation for sailing at a high school level.

Two versions of the Performance Sailing camp are offered: However, both focus on techniques used in sailboat racing and performance boat handing.

In the Performance Laser Sailing version, campers sail single-handed Lasers and gain insight into theory and technique to prepare them for any sailing that lies ahead. Campers in the Performance Capri Sailing version follow a similar curriculum while double handing Capris. Both versions, though, allow campers to take on the work needed to earn certification through US Sailing.

You’ll learn the following skills:
  • One design racing
  • Advanced Right of Way Rules
  • Helmsmanship, Heel Priority
  • The Starting Sequence
  • Sailing a Course
  • Strategies & Tactics
What to Bring

Students should bring comfortable clothes, a jacket or fleece, a towel and change of clothes, lots of sun protection, a lanyard to secure their glasses around their heads, a bottle of water, and shoes that stay on your feet and can get wet. (Personal lifejacket highly recommended.)

Important: On the first day of camp, please arrive 15-20 minutes before the start time to register and sign-in

Prerequistes: Age 11-16. Must be qualified to rent (hold a valid rental card) for the type of boat used in the camp (Capri or Laser, as appropriate for each session).
