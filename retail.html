Retail Shop

To help our customers on site, we offer a small retail and sundries shop stocked with essentials and goodies. To take care of unexpected needs, whether it’s ice cream and a snack or a dry shirt, we have the goods!

Although a small shop, we stock items from top name manufacturers, and are proud to carry their equipment and merchandise.

A Sample of What We Offer
  • Shoreline memorabilia and gifts like cards, hats, and t-shirts
  • Charts of local bird species
  • Kids’ toys like kites and beach toys
  • Binoculars
  • Personal items such as tissues, aspirin, and first-aid supplies
  • Candy, energy bars, soda, water, and ice cream
  • Tote bags, backpacks, purses, wallets
  • Sunglasses and straps
  • Wetsuits and booties
  • Clothing including fleece vests and pullovers
  • Windsurf Hawai`i and Da Kine harnesses
  • Windsurfing and sailing accessories and repair supplies
  • Lifejackets
  • Sunscreen and towels

While Shoreline can outfit you with accessories and gently used equipment for windsurfing, we do not offer new boards or sails. Two local distributors are:
