Weekly Lunch Pass *

Weekly Lunch Pass

The lunch pass provides your camper with lunch catered by the Lakeside Café all week for $40. Meals are served buffet style and include an entrée, beverage, fruit, and dessert. Each meal includes a vegetarian option and offers enough variety to satisfy even the pickiest eater! We can also accomodate food allergies.

Sample entrées include: Barbecue - hamburgers, hot dogs, and veggie burgers fresh off the grill; Build your own burrito - our staff makes burritos tailored to each individual camper, using their choice of fillings; and many more exciting options with a different entrée served each day.

Weekly Extended Care *

Weekly Extended Care

Also available is our extended care for those of you that may work or have conflicts dropping off/picking up your camper. It is available from 8:00am to 5:30pm for $140/week. During this time your camper can engage in their activity of choice in a supervised environment. Campers can choose from our selection of board- and card-games or bring their own books or other entertainment. Mornings are usually spent indoors, while afternoons will often see campers in extended care spending a portion of their time on pedalboats or kayaks on the water.
