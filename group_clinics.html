Private Group Clinics

Private group sailing clinic options are also available for companies and other organizations (for all skill levels, and focusing on enjoying the activity, while learning something new).

Beginner Clinic: Rigging, terminology...all of that can come later. With this Clinic, people who just want to get out on the water and relax in the sun with a calm breeze, can dip a toe (or a finger), and find out what all the fuss is about, including why sailing is so much fun

To sign up a group, and for more info, please fill out the Event Inquiry Form, or call the Events Team at 650-965-3779. To assess skill level, please contact our sailing staff at: sailing@shorelinelake.com
