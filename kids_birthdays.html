Kids Birthdays

Shoreline doesn't just take care of parties for big people. Kids (packages offered for ages 5-13) and their friends get the royal treatment from our Events Team too.

Whether it's a first birthday, or a fiesta for an older child, you can choose from a variety of Kids' Party Packages and options for entertainment and other activities (from pedalboating to facepainting, etc.) that will create a wonderful B-Day celebration for your special little guy or gal.

Please contact Shoreline’s Events Team for more information and to arrange the details for your child’s special day, by filling out the Event Inquiry Form, calling (650) 965-3779, or email: events@shorelinelake.com

Pricing

Minimum of 4 people – 3 hour clinic

  • 4-7 people: $170/person
  • 8-14 people: $150/person
  • 15-30 people: $125/person

To sign up a group, or for more info, please fill out the Events Inquiry Form, or call the Events Team at 650-965-3779. To assess skill level, please contact our sailing staff at: boathouse@shorelinelake.com.
